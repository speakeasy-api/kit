## Mechanical Acceptance

The broker exposes the sole public capability invocation entry point around the crate-private kernel
`invoke`. `BrokerInvocation::native` resolves an exact `NativeCatalog` identity and its schema;
`generic` and `external` require an already-resolved immutable `NormalizedSchema`; and `nested`
requires and installs a `DelegationSnapshot`. The real native direct and already-resolved generic
fixture paths are field-equal for the grant decision, complete `StoredEvent` values including trace,
`InvocationResult` including its `ReservationSnapshot`, and `UsageEnvelope`. Schema denials on both
paths produce zero events, reservations, commits, and dispatches.

Dynamic `ToolBinding` values pin a `NormalizedSchema`. Its validator is compiled once at schema
ingestion and shared across clones. The broker validates the exact pinned digest and arguments before
authorization, reservation, or dispatch. The successful invocation's exact reservation ID and spend
flow into the intent, debited `ReservationSnapshot`, ledger totals, and provider `UsageEnvelope`; a
pre-existing decoy reservation is not attributed.

Broker auth challenge and resolution records are durably bound to principal, project, run, attempt,
invocation, exact capability identity, schema digest, canonical request digest, grant-decision digest,
scope, typed credential handle, and trace. Challenge creation requires the exact persisted attempt
driver claim and occurs before reservation or kernel effects. Resolution accepts the same claim after
confirmed quiescence. The fault fixture performs exactly 100 clean cycles, reopening the SQLite store
and rebuilding cancellation, fence, budget, backend, and broker runtime for challenge, quiescent claim
resolution, and resumed invocation. Every cycle records one challenge and one resolution, dispatches
once, leaves zero reserved spend, commits exactly `(3 cost_microusd, 4 tokens, 0 turns, 1 tool, 0
processes)`, and reports the same reservation debit.

Arguments are bounded at 1 MiB, persisted auth records at 8 KiB, and auth scope at 512 bytes.
Malformed, oversized, mismatched, denied, corrupt, or conflicting state fails closed. The auth
challenge has no reservation or kernel side effect, changed principals, requests, grants, schemas, or
credentials cannot resolve it, and conflicting terminal resolutions are rejected.
