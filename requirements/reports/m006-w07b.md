  identity; descriptor and schema digests are pinned into immutable bindings.
- Tools, static resources, resource templates, and prompts remain distinct
  capability kinds. Server metadata, annotations, descriptions, links, and
  result text never become policy authority.
- Unsupported dialects and changed/unconfigured descriptors are unavailable or
  rejected; failed and cancelled refresh candidates do not publish partially.
- Every discovery page and invocation is broker-authorized. Raw AgentKit MCP
  construction and operations remain confined to the transport adapter.

  payloads; credential reflection is rejected before persistence.

  never reported as success. Exact durable terminal results may replay without
  another wire call or budget charge.
- List-change events are accepted only for an advertised kind whose negotiated
  `listChanged` value is exactly true. Receiver lag marks only those kinds.
- Reconnect replaces, rather than merges, server capabilities; stale connection
  and refresh generations cannot alter the current catalog.
