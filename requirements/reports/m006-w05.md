## Mechanical Acceptance

Authorization occurs before matching. Unauthorized entries are absent from search and inspection,
with no hidden count or differentiated denial. Search is bounded and deterministic. Inspection gives
the same absence for forged, forbidden, and unknown handles. The enforced discovery sequence is
search, inspect, then bind.

The binding ID is the full SHA-256 output over the exact tuple of capability identity, including
source, normalized input-schema digest, and authorization-snapshot digest. A binding separately pins
the original `Arc<CatalogEntry>`, entry digest, and authorized catalog-view digest. Changes to the
visible catalog, schema, grant, configuration, run, delegation, or request policy expire validation
with the single typed `BindingExpired` result. The old binding never mutates. Hidden-only catalog
changes do not perturb authorized handles or the authorized catalog view, and successful validation
returns the original pinned `Arc`.

Discovery has no broker, store, budget, or backend effects.
