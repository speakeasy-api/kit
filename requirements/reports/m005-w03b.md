## W03b Mechanical Acceptance

The local harness establishes the following exact W03b mechanical acceptance:

- syntax facts are always classified as syntactic Tree-sitter facts and never masquerade as
  semantic facts or semantic references;
- accepted-response and accepted-notification provenance is bound to the workspace revision, server
  generation, and document epoch, with stale or mismatched provenance rejected before normalization;
- every supported `WorkspaceEdit` shape lowers to canonical edit IR or returns a typed rejection;
  normalization itself performs no workspace writes; and
- mixed in-scope/outside-workspace edits and outside-path, hostile-URI, and symlink-swap attacks
  reject the whole operation with zero workspace or outside-target writes.

Supported `WorkspaceEdit` input is the `changes` text-edit map or ordered `documentChanges` containing
versioned text edits and create, rename, or delete resource operations, with bounded change
annotations. Successful lowering preserves representable operation order and canonical IR
round-trips.

## Fail-Closed Unsupported Subset

The following unsupported subset is rejected rather than approximated:

- edit-bearing methods other than `textDocument/rename`, `workspace/willCreateFiles`,
  `workspace/willRenameFiles`, and `workspace/willDeleteFiles`;
- payloads containing both `changes` and `documentChanges`, missing versions on
  `documentChanges` text edits, empty or no-effective edits, overlapping edits, repeated path
  claims, and other unrepresentable ordering;
- confirmation-required or unresolved annotations; create/rename overwrite or ignore-if-exists
  semantics; and recursive or ignore-if-not-exists delete semantics;
- malformed or unknown wire fields, non-file or non-canonical URIs, unknown, dirty, conflicting, or
  outside-workspace paths, and stale revision, generation, document-epoch, or document-version data;
  and
- inputs exceeding snapshot, operation, content, URI, position-work, or retained-output bounds.
